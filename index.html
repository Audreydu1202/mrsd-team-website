About the team

From algorithms to integrated robotic systems.

Our backgrounds span computer science, electrical and computer engineering, mechatronics, biomedical engineering, and robotics. Across academic laboratories and industry, we have worked on vision, 3D reconstruction, autonomous navigation, human–robot interaction, manipulation learning, connected autonomous vehicles, and warehouse robotics.

We are prepared to tackle robotics problems that require disciplined integration across sensing, decision-making, control, mechanical systems, and reliable software—especially manipulation, autonomous systems, humanoid robotics, and robot learning.
